# Gaussian Functions

- Gaussian functions are stable under pointwise and convolution products. The Fourier transform interleaves these two algebraic structures [src](https://x.com/gabrielpeyre/status/1851504307088277700)

![](2_concepts/media/Pasted%20image%2020241031124234.png)

- The FT turns convolution into product. On Gaussians, FT inverse the covariance so it turns sum into inverse of sum of inverses
