# Orthonormal Vectors and Matrices

- Portmanteau for orthogonal and normal

- Orthogonal: two vectors $u$ and $v$ are orthogonal if
    - $u \cdot v = u^\top v = 0$
- Normal: a vector $u$ is normalized it has unit length ie:
        - $||u||= \sqrt{u \cdot u} = 1$
        - $u^\top u = 1$ and $v^\top v = 1$
