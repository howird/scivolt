# Orthogonal Matrices

- A square matrix $R$ whose row (or column) vectors are [orthonormal](2_concepts/orthonormal.md)
### Properties

- Inverse is equal to its transpose: $R^{-1} = R^\top$
- Multiplication with its inverse/transpose is the identity: $R R^\top = R^\top R = I$
