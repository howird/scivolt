# Mean Squared Error

## Relation to Estimators

- Estimators: Functions or rules that provide estimates of population attributes based on sample data.
- Goal: Use sample statistics to make accurate and reliable estimates of the unknown population attributes.

## Summary

A population attribute is a characteristic that quantifies some aspect of the entire population. These attributes are central to statistical analysis because they represent the true values we aim to estimate using sample data. Understanding and estimating population attributes enable statisticians to infer information about the population, conduct hypothesis tests, and make predictions.
