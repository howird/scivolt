#### examples of both types of tasks:
##### Compound Tasks:

###### Project

- something that has a due date and consists of various 

###### Event
- something you must be there for, that will require you to do something before and/or after

- examples:
    - 1) you are a professor, when you schedule a lecture, which is an event (compound) task, it also creates two atomic tasks:
        - prep-work: creating and reviewing lecture slides
        - follow-up: find answers to questions that you were unable to answer during class, and post a class announcement with the clarifications
    - 2) you are a consultant, when you schedule a client meeting, which is an event (compound) task, it also creates two atomic tasks:
        - prep-work: creating and reviewing presentation on work done to date
        - follow-up: consolodate and define client needs in document for the team

###### Deliverable

- atomic task: you have to do one thing
- ex:
    - prep, see compound>event
