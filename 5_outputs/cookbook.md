## Thit Kho Caramelized Pork with Eggs

https://www.instagram.com/p/C7UtXo1y5BX/
https://www.instagram.com/p/C6uBd0XSTg6/
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=clSi0Q1FpS8&list=PLB0EZ6nMXq29RXVMm3gAspY9FWwHl5A3W&index=7

### Ingredients

#### Main

- 3 lbs pork belly or pork spare ribs, cut into cubes or 2-inch pieces
- 12 hard-boiled chicken eggs or 2 cups canned quail eggs

#### Caramelization
- 1/3 cup sugar
- 2 tbsp oil

#### Aromatics
- 2 tbsp minced garlic
- 3 tbsp chopped shallots

#### Seasoning and Liquids

- 1/4 cup fish sauce
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 12 oz coconut water or coconut soda (substitute with 7-Up or cola if preferred)
- 2 tbsp dark soy sauce (optional for deeper color and flavor)
- 1 tsp garlic powder (optional)
- 1 tsp onion powder (optional)
- 1 tsp paprika (optional)
- 1 tsp MSG (optional)

#### Garnish
- 3 tbsp green onions, sliced

---

### Instructions

#### Step 1: Prepare the Meat
1. If using pork belly, parboil the meat in salted water for 6-8 minutes to remove impurities, then rinse and set aside.

#### Step 2: Caramelize the Sugar
1. In a pot, heat oil over medium heat and add sugar, stirring continuously until it turns a golden brown caramel color. Be careful not to burn the sugar; if it does burn, discard it and start again.

#### Step 3: Add Meat and Aromatics
1. Add the pork pieces to the pot, stirring to coat the meat in the caramelized sugar.
2. Cook until the meat is browned, then add garlic and shallots. Cook for another 2-3 minutes until fragrant.

#### Step 4: Add Seasoning and Liquid Ingredients
1. Add fish sauce, black pepper, coconut water or soda, and dark soy sauce. If desired, add garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, and MSG for extra flavor.
2. Stir to combine, then cover and bring to a simmer.

#### Step 5: Simmer
1. If using pork belly, simmer on low heat for 1 hour, skimming off scum as needed. For pork spare ribs, simmer for about 20 minutes, stirring occasionally to maintain the caramel color.

#### Step 6: Add Eggs and Final Cook
1. Uncover and add the hard-boiled eggs or quail eggs. Continue to simmer for another 5-30 minutes, depending on meat tenderness and egg flavor absorption.
2. Flip the eggs occasionally to coat them in the sauce.

#### Step 7: Finish and Garnish
1. Skim excess oil from the top if necessary, taste, and adjust the seasoning.
2. Garnish with green onions and serve with steamed rice and optional sliced cucumbers.

## Lemongrass Chicken

https://www.instagram.com/p/C72JWA_hN14/

### Ingredients

#### Marinade
- 3 lbs chicken thighs, trimmed of excess fat/skin
- 4 tbsp minced garlic
- 3 tbsp minced lemongrass
- 5 tbsp oyster sauce
- 2-3 tbsp brown sugar
- 2 tbsp soy sauce (or seasoning sauce)
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

#### Scallion Oil
- 1 cup chopped scallions
- Salt, to taste
- 1 tbsp oil (e.g., avocado oil)

---

### Instructions

#### Step 1: Marinate the Chicken
1. In a bowl, combine all marinade ingredients: garlic, lemongrass, oyster sauce, brown sugar, soy sauce, and black pepper.
2. Add the chicken thighs, ensuring they are well-coated with the marinade. Cover and marinate overnight in the refrigerator for maximum flavor.

#### Step 2: Cook the Chicken
1. Heat a skillet over medium heat. Place marinated chicken thighs in the skillet.
2. **Cooking Time**:
- For bone-in chicken thighs: Cook for 12-15 minutes on each side, until the internal temperature reaches 165°F.
- For boneless chicken thighs: Cook for 7-8 minutes on each side.
3. Adjust cooking time based on your stove's strength and the number of chicken thighs in the skillet.

#### Step 3: Prepare the Scallion Oil
1. In a microwave-safe bowl, combine chopped scallions, salt, and oil.
2. Microwave for 1-2 minutes, or until the scallions are softened and infused with the oil.

#### Step 4: Serve
1. Plate the cooked chicken and drizzle with scallion oil.
2. Serve with accompaniments such as cucumbers, tomatoes, fried egg, and rice for a complete meal.

Enjoy this flavorful, aromatic lemongrass chicken with a side of fresh and vibrant toppings!


## Chicken Congee (Rice Porridge)

https://www.instagram.com/p/C9V5UIKy4ro/

### Ingredients

#### Main Ingredients
- 1 lb chicken thighs (450 g), bone-in or boneless
- 1 inch ginger, whole (40 g)
- 2 stalks green onion, diced (30 g)
- 1 tsp chicken bouillon powder (5 g)
- 4 cups chicken bone broth (960 g) or chicken stock
- 1 cup white short-grain rice (150 g) or preferred rice type

#### Toppings

- 1 tbsp sesame oil (12 g), or to taste
- 1/2 tsp white pepper powder (2.5 g), or to taste
- 1 stalk green onion (15 g), chopped, or to taste

---

### Instructions

#### Step 1: Prepare the Rice and Broth
1. Wash the rice thoroughly and place it in the rice cooker bowl.
2. Add chicken bone broth or chicken stock to the pot, followed by ginger, diced green onions, and chicken bouillon powder. Stir to combine.

#### Step 2: Add the Chicken
1. Place the chicken thighs directly on top of the rice and broth mixture in the rice cooker.

#### Step 3: Cook the Congee
1. Set the rice cooker to cook for 60 minutes. If you have a Zojirushi rice cooker, use the "Porridge" mode (68 minutes).

#### Step 4: Shred the Chicken
1. Once the rice cooker is done, remove the cooked chicken thighs, shred them, and return the shredded chicken to the congee.

#### Step 5: Serve and Add Toppings
1. Serve the congee in bowls, topped with sesame oil, white pepper powder, and chopped green onions to taste.
2. Additional topping ideas: chili oil, soy sauce, fish sauce, or fried garlic for an extra layer of flavor.

Enjoy this warm and comforting chicken congee, perfect for any time of day!

https://www.instagram.com/p/C3QuNVevHU4/

- **Pork**:
- 1 1/2 lbs pork butt or shoulder, cut into 4 equal chunks (675 g)

- **Marinade**:

- 1 inch ginger, sliced (20 g)
- 2 cloves garlic, minced (6 g)
- 1 tbsp five spice powder (10 g)
- 1 tsp white pepper powder (2 g)

- **Glaze**:
- 1 tbsp honey (21 g), just enough to glaze

### Instructions

1. Place pork chunks in a mixing bowl. Add ginger, garlic, char siu sauce, five spice powder, white pepper powder, and soy sauce (if using)
2. Mix thoroughly to coat the pork in the marinade. Cover and refrigerate, allowing the pork to marinate for at least a few hours, preferably overnight for best flavor.
3. Air-fry at 400C for 15 minutes.
    - Flip the pork every 2-3 minutes and glaze with honey
5. let rest, and slice into thin pieces

## Canh Mong Toi

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ms3Xm_idZNE

- **Prep Time**: 15 minutes
- **Cooking Time**: 30 minutes

### Ingredients

#### Broth
- 3 quarts water
- 1 bundle Egyptian spinach, washed and cut into strips
- 1/4 cup dried shrimp, rehydrated in warm water
- 1 small shallot, minced
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 tbsp MSG (optional)
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 2 tbsp fish sauce
- Salt and pepper, to taste

#### Shrimp Meatballs
- 1 lb shrimp, minced
- 1 tsp fish sauce
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

#### Garnishes
- Green onions, chopped

---

### Directions

#### Step 1: Prepare the Dried Shrimp and Broth Base
1. Rehydrate the dried shrimp in warm water for a few minutes, then set aside.
2. In a stock pot, heat oil over medium heat and sauté the minced shallots until fragrant.
3. Add the rehydrated dried shrimp and sauté for an additional minute.

#### Step 2: Make the Broth
1. Add 3 quarts of water to the pot and bring to a boil, skimming off any impurities.
2. Add the Egyptian spinach, massaging the leaves lightly before adding to release the sticky residue, which helps thicken the broth.
3. Season with fish sauce, MSG (if using), sugar, and salt to taste.

#### Step 3: Prepare the Shrimp Meatballs
1. In a mixing bowl, combine minced shrimp, fish sauce, and black pepper. Form small meatballs using a spoon.
2. Gently add the shrimp meatballs to the soup pot. They are done cooking once they float to the surface.

#### Step 4: Serve
1. Garnish the soup with chopped green onions and serve hot.

## Easy Hainanese Chicken Rice

https://www.instagram.com/p/C59LsBdSkwr/

### Ingredients

#### Chicken
- 2 chicken thighs (450 g)
- 2 tsp sesame oil (5 g)
- 1/4 tsp salt (1.5 g)

#### Rice
- 2 cups jasmine rice (370 g)
- 6 cloves garlic, minced or grated (10 g)
- 1/2 inch ginger, minced or grated (10 g)
- 2 green onions, chopped (20 g)
- 2 cups chicken broth (480 g)
- 1 tsp chicken bouillon powder (5 g)
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder (optional, 5 g)

#### Ginger Scallion Sauce
- 1/2 inch ginger, minced or grated (10 g)
- 1 green onion, minced (10 g)
- Salt, to taste
- 1 tsp sesame oil (3 g)
- 3 tbsp neutral oil (30 g)

#### Chili Sauce
- 6 cloves garlic, minced or grated (10 g)
- 1/2 inch ginger, minced or grated (10 g)
- 2 tbsp sambal (25 g)
- 2 tbsp sriracha (25 g)
- 2 tbsp chicken broth (20 g)
- 1 tbsp lime juice (5 g)
- 2 tsp sugar (9 g)

#### Optional Toppings
- 1 cucumber, sliced (100 g)
- Cilantro, to taste
- Green onion, to taste

---

### Directions

#### Step 1: Prepare the Chicken
1. In a mixing bowl, coat the chicken thighs with sesame oil and salt. Set aside.

#### Step 2: Prepare the Rice
1. In the rice cooker bowl, add jasmine rice, garlic, ginger, green onion, chicken broth, chicken bouillon, and turmeric powder (if using).

#### Step 3: Combine Chicken and Rice
1. Place the seasoned chicken thighs on top of the rice mixture in the rice cooker.

#### Step 4: Cook
1. Run the rice cooker on the white rice setting until fully cooked.

#### Step 5: Prepare Ginger Scallion Sauce
1. In a bowl, add minced ginger, green onion, and salt.
2. In a small pan, heat sesame oil and neutral oil until shimmering or lightly smoking.
3. Carefully pour the hot oil over the ginger scallion mixture, stirring to combine.

#### Step 6: Prepare Chili Sauce
1. In a separate bowl, mix garlic, ginger, sambal, sriracha, chicken broth, lime juice, and sugar until well combined.

#### Step 7: Serve
1. When the rice cooker is done, remove the chicken and slice it or leave it whole as desired.
2. Serve a portion of ginger-garlic rice with the chicken, accompanied by ginger scallion sauce, chili sauce, and optional toppings like cucumber slices, cilantro, and green onion. Enjoy!

## Crispy Quesadilla with One-Pot Roast and Cilantro Sauce

https://www.instagram.com/p/C6edfKzuRXU/

### Ingredients
#### Roast
- 3 lbs chuck roast
- 1/2 large yellow onion, sliced
- 4-5 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp red curry paste (or your favorite curry paste)
- 1.5 tbsp sweet paprika
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 18 oz bone broth (e.g., Kettle and Fire)

#### Cilantro Sauce
- 1 large handful of cilantro
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1/2 jalapeño (adjust to taste)
- 3 heaping tbsp plain yogurt
- Juice of 1 large lime
- Salt and pepper, to taste

#### Quesadilla
- 2 oz shredded roast (per quesadilla)
- 1 low-carb tortilla (or your favorite tortilla)
- Cheese, divided (half serving per side)

#### Optional Toppings
- Sliced jalapeños
- Fresh cilantro
- Sliced avocado
- Chopped onion
- Cilantro sauce (for dipping)

### Instructions

#### Step 1: Make the One-Pot Roast
1. **Preheat** oven to 325°F (163°C).
2. **Prepare the Roast**: In an oven-safe dish or pot with a lid, add sliced onion and chuck roast.
3. **Season**: Add minced garlic, curry paste, sweet paprika, salt, and pepper. Pour in the bone broth.
4. **Roast**: Cover and cook in the oven for 3-4 hours. Remove, shred the meat with two forks, and return to the oven at 400°F (200°C) for an additional 1-2 hours for enhanced flavor and tenderness.

#### Step 2: Prepare the Cilantro Sauce
1. **Combine**: In a food processor, blend cilantro, garlic, jalapeño, yogurt, lime juice, salt, and pepper.
2. **Adjust** to taste and set aside.

#### Step 3: Make the Quesadilla
1. **Heat Pan**: Bring a pan to medium heat.
2. **Assemble Quesadilla**: Add half of the cheese to the pan. When it starts to bubble, place the tortilla on top, add 2 oz of shredded roast, and flip.
3. **Finish**: Add the remaining cheese on top, fold, and cook until the outside is crispy and golden.

#### Step 4: Serve
1. **Top** with desired toppings (jalapeños, cilantro, avocado, onion, etc.).
2. **Dip** into cilantro sauce and enjoy!

## Steamed Shrimp Rice Paper Dumplings

https://www.instagram.com/p/C4d6fyGPGVu/

### Ingredients

#### Dumplings
- 1 lb raw peeled and deveined shrimp, cut into small pieces then rough chopped
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 2 tsp fresh ginger, minced
- 2 tsp cornstarch
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 2 tsp sesame oil
- 2 tsp soy sauce
- ~20 sheets rice paper
- Parchment paper
- Avocado oil spray

#### Sauce
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- 1 tbsp chili oil or chili garlic sauce (optional)
- 1/4 tsp sugar

### Instructions

#### Step 1: Prepare the Shrimp Filling
1. In a bowl, combine the shrimp, garlic, ginger, cornstarch, salt, pepper, sesame oil, and soy sauce. Mix until well combined.

#### Step 2: Prepare the Rice Paper
1. Cut the dry rice paper sheets into quarters with scissors (some cracking is okay).
2. Fill a large bowl with water and place two damp paper towels on a cutting board.
3. Stack 3 quarter sheets of rice paper, rotating slightly for irregular edges, and soak in the water for 30 seconds until soft but still slightly firm.
4. Place the softened stack on the damp paper towel on the cutting board, where it will continue to soften slightly.

#### Step 3: Assemble the Dumplings
1. Place 2 tsp of shrimp filling in the center of each rice paper stack.
2. Fold the edges across to meet at the top, pinching to seal (like wrapping a basket).

#### Step 4: Prepare for Steaming
1. Line a plate that fits within a bamboo steamer or large pot with parchment paper and spray with avocado oil.
2. In a wok or pan that fits the steamer, add 1-2 inches of water and bring to a boil.
3. Place the parchment-lined plate with dumplings into the steamer and cover with the lid.

#### Step 5: Steam the Dumplings
1. Steam for 10 minutes, then allow to cool for a few minutes to set. (If using a pot instead of a bamboo steamer, place the parchment-lined plate on top of three crumpled foil balls to keep it above the water line.)

#### Step 6: Prepare the Dipping Sauce
1. In a small bowl, mix soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, sesame seeds, chili oil or chili garlic sauce (if desired), and sugar.

#### Step 7: Serve
1. Serve the dumplings warm with the dipping sauce. Enjoy!


## Chicken Shawarma Rice Bowl

https://www.instagram.com/p/C52BK34RT_X/

### Ingredients

#### Golden Rice
- 2 cups basmati rice
- 3 cups water
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 2 tbsp olive oil

#### Lemony Garlic Yogurt Sauce
- 1 cup Greek yogurt
- Zest of 1 lemon
- Juice of 1/4 lemon
- 2 cloves garlic, pressed
- 3 tbsp olive oil

#### Pickled Red Cabbage
- 1/4 red cabbage, thinly sliced
- 1 cup water
- 1 cup white vinegar
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp maple syrup (optional)

#### Shawarma Spice Blend
- 1 tbsp cumin
- 1 tbsp paprika
- 1 tbsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp coriander seed
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp ground cloves
- 1/2 tsp ground nutmeg
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp chili flakes (adjust to taste)
- Salt, to taste
- Neutral oil (for coating chicken)

#### Additional Bowl Ingredients
- Sliced tomatoes
- Sliced cucumbers
- Cooked chicken shawarma (using the spice blend above)

---

### Instructions

#### Step 1: Prepare the Golden Rice
1. Wash the basmati rice thoroughly and drain.
2. In a bowl, combine rice with olive oil and turmeric, mixing well to coat.
3. In a pot, add 3 cups of water, then add the rice mixture, cinnamon stick, and bay leaves.
4. Bring to a boil, then lower to a simmer, cover, and cook until water is absorbed and rice is tender.

#### Step 2: Make the Lemony Garlic Yogurt Sauce
1. In a small pan, heat olive oil and sauté the garlic briefly until softened, removing the raw pungent flavor.
2. In a bowl, combine Greek yogurt, lemon zest, lemon juice, and the sautéed garlic with olive oil. Mix well and set aside.

#### Step 3: Prepare the Pickled Red Cabbage
1. In a small pot, combine water, white vinegar, salt, and maple syrup (if using). Bring to a boil.
2. Pour the hot pickling liquid over the thinly sliced red cabbage and let sit for at least 15 minutes or until cool.

#### Step 4: Cook the Chicken Shawarma
1. In a bowl, mix together the shawarma spices. Coat the chicken with neutral oil, then add the spice blend and toss to coat evenly.
2. Season with salt and grill or pan-cook until the chicken is cooked through, with a nice char on the outside.

#### Step 5: Assemble the Shawarma Rice Bowl
1. Place a serving of golden rice in each bowl.
2. Add grilled chicken shawarma, sliced tomatoes, cucumbers, and pickled red cabbage.
3. Drizzle with the lemony garlic yogurt sauce, and enjoy!
